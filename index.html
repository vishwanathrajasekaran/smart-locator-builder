Frequently asked questions

What is Smart Locator Builder?

A free browser tool that turns pasted HTML or a DevTools screenshot into ranked, validated XPath and CSS locators.

Is my HTML uploaded anywhere?

No. Parsing, locator generation, validation and screenshot reading (OCR) all run in your browser.

Which automation frameworks are supported?

Selenium with C# and Java, Playwright with TypeScript and Java, plus generic XPath and CSS.

Does a high score guarantee a locator will keep working?

No. Validation covers only the snapshot you provide, and scores are guidance, not a guarantee against future DOM changes.
